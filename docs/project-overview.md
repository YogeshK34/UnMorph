# UnMorph
### Decentralized Image Integrity & Authentication Layer

UnMorph is a decentralized security layer designed to protect digital images against unauthorized manipulation, morphing, and AI-generated alterations.

As generative AI makes it increasingly easy to manipulate photographs, create convincing deepfakes, and alter visual evidence, establishing the authenticity of digital images is becoming a critical challenge.

UnMorph addresses this problem by combining cryptographic verification, AI-powered image tampering detection, human verification, and decentralized infrastructure. The platform allows users to register original images, establish verifiable proof of their integrity, and subsequently verify whether an image has been altered or manipulated.

By leveraging World ID, Arkiv, and 0G, UnMorph creates a trust infrastructure for digital images:

- World ID: Provides privacy-preserving proof that an image submission or registration is associated with a verified unique human, where required.
- Arkiv: Maintains queryable, wallet-owned records of image fingerprints, ownership references, verification metadata, and integrity history.
- 0G: Provides decentralized storage for image assets and supporting evidence, with decentralized compute infrastructure available for AI-powered image analysis.

At its core, UnMorph aims to make image manipulation detectable and image authenticity independently verifiable, creating a stronger foundation of trust for digital photographs, visual evidence, identity documents, media, and online content.

The long-term vision is to establish a universal image integrity layer that helps people, organizations, and digital platforms distinguish authentic visual content from manipulated imagery.
