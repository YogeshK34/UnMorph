JSON-RPC over POST. Pass the key in the path, or as a header.

Path
curl --json '{"jsonrpc":"2.0","id":1,"method":"eth_blockNumber","params":[]}' \
  https://rpc.rabdi.db-chain.testnet.arkiv.network/ark_live_rDIK…EKMy
X-API-KEY header
curl --json '{"jsonrpc":"2.0","id":1,"method":"eth_blockNumber","params":[]}' \
  -H "X-API-KEY: ark_live_rDIK…EKMy" \
  https://rpc.rabdi.db-chain.testnet.arkiv.network
Authorization: Bearer
curl --json '{"jsonrpc":"2.0","id":1,"method":"eth_blockNumber","params":[]}' \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer ark_live_rDIK…EKMy" \
  https://rpc.rabdi.db-chain.testnet.arkiv.network
